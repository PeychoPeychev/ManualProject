7. Recommendation

❌ Release is NOT recommended at this stage.

The application contains several Major defects related to cart behavior and mobile UI that should be addressed before a production release.

8. Conclusion

Manual testing of the Sauce Demo Shopify website was successfully completed for the defined scope.

Further testing cycles are recommended after defect resolution.
